The Hughes Amendment and the Ambiguity in 18 U.S.C. § 922(o)

By Daniel · Directed research, ~9,000 words

This page is a template — replace the paragraph below with the actual text of the paper (or an embedded PDF/download link), then duplicate this file for each new post.

This piece takes up 18 U.S.C. § 922(o) — the federal machine-gun transfer bar commonly known as the Hughes Amendment — and asks a narrow question: is the statute's language genuinely ambiguous in a way that would justify legislative attention, independent of any view on what the law should say? The analysis is deliberately framed as a question of "can," not "should," to keep the statutory reading separate from the policy debate.
